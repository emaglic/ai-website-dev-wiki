# Open Questions

This page tracks unresolved questions that affect strategy, content, design, technical implementation, and deployment.

## Project Strategy Questions

- What kind of website is being built?
- Who is the website for?
- What is the primary goal?
- Who is the target audience?

## Content Questions

- What raw content has been provided?
- What content is missing?
- What content needs rewriting, verification, or approval?

## Design Questions

- What visual tone should the site have?
- What inspiration sources should guide the design?
- Are there brand assets, color constraints, typography preferences, or imagery requirements?

## Technical Questions

- What stack should be used?
- Does the project need a framework, build process, CMS, database, forms, ecommerce, search, or integrations?
- Should the site remain FTP-friendly?

## Deployment Questions

- Where will the site be hosted?
- Are there domain, analytics, form handling, email, SSL, or performance requirements?

## Notes For Future Agents

- Close questions when they are answered and move durable answers into the appropriate wiki article.
- Keep unresolved questions specific enough that the user can answer them easily.
